# Aftercare Instructions

These are general instructions. If something doesn't feel right, call the clinic.

## After a tooth extraction
- Bite on the gauze for 30 to 45 minutes.
- Do not rinse, spit forcefully, drink through a straw or smoke for 24 hours.
- Eat soft, cool foods for the first day and chew on the other side.
- Mild pain and swelling for 2 to 3 days is normal. Contact us if bleeding doesn't stop after a few hours, or if pain gets worse after day 3.

## After teeth whitening
- Avoid coffee, tea, red wine, cola, curry and smoking for 48 hours.
- Some sensitivity for 24 to 48 hours is normal; use a sensitive toothpaste.

## After a filling
- If you had numbing, wait until the numbness wears off before eating (usually 2 to 3 hours).
- Tooth-colored fillings are fully set when you leave the clinic.
- Mild sensitivity to cold for a week or two is normal.

## After a root canal
- The tooth may be tender for a few days; over-the-counter pain relief usually helps.
- Avoid chewing hard food on that tooth until your permanent crown is placed.
