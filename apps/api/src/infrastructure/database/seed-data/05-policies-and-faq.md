# Policies and Frequently Asked Questions

## Booking and cancellations
- Appointment requests made online or through the website assistant are confirmed by our team by phone within one business day.
- Please give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations and missed appointments may be charged $50.
- If you are more than 15 minutes late we may need to reschedule.

## New patients
New patients are welcome, including children from age 2. Please arrive 10 minutes early to fill in a short medical history form, or complete it online using the link in your confirmation text.

## Dental emergencies
We keep same-day slots for emergencies such as severe toothache, a broken or knocked-out tooth, a lost crown or swelling. Call (555) 014-2290 as early as possible. If you have facial swelling that affects breathing or swallowing, uncontrolled bleeding, or a head injury, go to the nearest emergency room.

## Anxious patients
We offer longer appointments, noise-cancelling headphones and nitrous oxide (laughing gas) for nervous patients. Let us know when booking.
