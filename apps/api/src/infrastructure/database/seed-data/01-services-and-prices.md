# Services and Prices

All prices are in US dollars and are for patients without insurance. Insured patients usually pay less; see "Insurance and Payment".

## Preventive care
- New patient exam with full X-rays: $149 (about 60 minutes)
- Routine checkup and exam: $79
- Professional cleaning (scale and polish): $119, takes about 45 minutes
- Deep cleaning for gum disease: from $220 per quadrant
- Children's checkup and cleaning (under 14): $89
- Fluoride treatment: $35

## Cosmetic dentistry
- In-office teeth whitening (Zoom, one 90-minute visit): $449
- Take-home whitening kit with custom trays: $249
- Porcelain veneers: from $950 per tooth
- Cosmetic bonding: from $280 per tooth

## Restorative treatment
- Tooth-colored filling: $150 to $260 depending on size
- Root canal, front tooth: $780
- Root canal, molar: $1,150
- Crown (porcelain): $1,200
- Simple extraction: $180
- Wisdom tooth extraction: from $320 per tooth
- Dental implant (implant, abutment and crown): from $3,400

## Orthodontics
- Braces and clear aligner consultation: free (30 minutes)
- Clear aligners (Invisalign): from $3,900
- Metal braces: from $3,600

## Emergency care
- Emergency exam with X-ray: $99, credited toward treatment done the same day.
