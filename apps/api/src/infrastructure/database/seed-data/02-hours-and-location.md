# Opening Hours and Location

## Opening hours
- Monday to Friday: 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM
- Saturday: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- Sunday: closed
- Public holidays: closed, except for emergency appointments by phone

The last appointment of the day starts 45 minutes before closing.

## Address
SmileCare Dental Clinic, Suite 210, 48 Maple Grove Avenue, Riverside. We are on the second floor of the Maple Grove Medical Centre, above the pharmacy. The building has an elevator and is wheelchair accessible.

## Parking and transport
Free parking for patients is available in the building's underground garage (levels B1 and B2). Bring your ticket to reception for validation. The Maple Grove bus stop (routes 12 and 31) is a two-minute walk away.
