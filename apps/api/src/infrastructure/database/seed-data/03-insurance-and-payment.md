# Insurance and Payment

## Accepted insurance
We are in-network with Delta Dental (PPO), Cigna Dental, MetLife, Aetna Dental and Guardian. We can submit claims to most other PPO plans as an out-of-network provider. We do not currently accept HMO/DHMO plans or Medicaid.

Please bring your insurance card and photo ID to your first visit. We check your benefits before treatment and give you a written estimate of what you will owe.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, HSA and FSA cards. We do not accept personal checks.

## Payment plans
- Treatments over $500 can be split into 3, 6 or 12 monthly payments with 0% interest through CareCredit (subject to approval).
- Orthodontic treatment can be paid monthly over the length of treatment.

## Membership plan (no insurance)
The SmileCare Membership costs $299 per year for adults and $219 for children. It includes two checkups, two cleanings, all routine X-rays, one emergency exam, and 15% off all other treatment.
